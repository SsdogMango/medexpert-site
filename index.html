Project Description

Our group designed an app to streamline the prescription process by enabling prescribing doctors to view real-time medication inventory at local pharmacies before sending a prescription. By giving healthcare providers immediate access to inventory information, the app helps ensure that patients can promptly obtain their medications without the frustration of visiting multiple pharmacies or dealing with time-consuming prescription transfers. This solution aims to reduce delays in treatment, improve patient satisfaction, and enhance communication between doctors and pharmacies, ultimately making the prescription fulfillment process faster, more efficient, and more reliable.

Benefits to the Company

  • Improved data accuracy
  • Time savings on reporting
